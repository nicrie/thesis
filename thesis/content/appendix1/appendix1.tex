\section{Frobenius Norm of the Cross-Covariance Matrix}\label{app:proof:frobenius_norm}
The total squared covariance between $\bm{X} \in \mathtt{C}^{N \times P}$ and $\bm{Y}\in \mathtt{C}^{N \times Q}$ is quantified using the Frobenius norm $\Vab{\cdot}_F$ of the cross-covariance matrix $\bm{C} = \bm{X}^H \bm{Y} / (N-1)$:

\begin{align}\label{eq:app:frobenius_norm}
    \Vab{\bm{C}}_F &\equiv  \pab{\sum_i^N \sum_j^P \vab{c_{ij}}^2}^{1/2} = \pab{\sum_{i}^{\min(N, P)} \sigma_i^2(\bm{C})}^{1/2}.
\end{align}

For the proof of Equation~\eqref{eq:app:frobenius_norm}, consider the singular value decomposition of $\bm{C}$,

\begin{align}
    \bm{C} &= \bm{V}_x \bm{\Sigma} \bm{V}_y^H,
\end{align}

where $\bm{V}_x \in \mathtt{C}^{P \times P}$ and $\bm{V}_y \in \mathtt{C}^{Q \times Q}$ are unitary matrices, and $\bm{\Sigma} \in \mathtt{C}^{P \times Q}$ is a diagonal matrix with singular values $\sigma_i(\bm{C})$ on the diagonal.

Using the orthonormality of $\bm{V}_x$ and $\bm{V}_y$ as well as the property that the trace of a matrix remains invariant under cyclic permutations, we find:

\begin{align*}
    \Vab{\bm{C}}_F^2 &= \Vab{\bm{V}_x\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}_y^T}_F^2 \\
    &= \tr \pab{\pab{\bm{V}_x\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}_y^T}^T\pab{\bm{V}_x\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}_y^T}} \\
    &= \tr \pab{\bm{V}_y\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}_x^T\bm{V}_x\bm{\Sigma}\bm{V}_y^T} \\
    &= \tr \pab{\bm{V}_y\bm{\Sigma}^2\bm{V}_y^T} \\
    &= \tr \pab{\bm{\Sigma}^2} \\
    &= \sum_{r=1}^k \sigma_r^2.
\end{align*}
