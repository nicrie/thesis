Class of pattern recognition techniques that typically require a metric to be defined that measures the similarity of
any two vectors in input space, and are generally fast to "train" but slow at making predictions for test data points.

\section{Introduction to Gaussian Processes}
Definition and Overview: Explain what Gaussian Processes are, their significance in machine learning, and their use in modeling continuous data.
Basic Concepts: Introduce key concepts such as Gaussian distributions, covariance functions, and kernels.
\section{Gaussian Processes for Regression}
GP Regression Model: Describe the formulation of GP for regression tasks, including prior, likelihood, and posterior distributions.
Kernel Functions: Discuss common kernel functions (e.g., squared exponential, Matérn) and their role in defining the GP's properties.
Hyperparameter Tuning: Explain how to tune hyperparameters using techniques like maximum likelihood estimation.
\section{Spatial Modeling with Gaussian Processes}
Spatial Data: Define spatial data and the importance of modeling spatial dependencies.
GPs in Spatial Modeling: Explain how GPs are particularly suited for spatial data due to their ability to model complex, non-linear relationships and provide uncertainty estimates.
\subsection{Log Gaussian Cox Process}
Introduction to Cox Processes: Explain Cox processes and their relevance in spatial point process modeling.
Log Gaussian Cox Model: Describe the log Gaussian Cox model, which assumes the log intensity function is modeled by a GP.
Mathematical Formulation: Provide the mathematical formulation of the log Gaussian Cox model.
\subsection{Negative Binomial Distribution}
Introduction to Negative Binomial Distribution: Explain the negative binomial distribution and its use in modeling overdispersed count data.
Integration with Log Gaussian Cox Model: Describe how the negative binomial distribution can be integrated into the log Gaussian Cox model to handle count data with overdispersion.
\section{Model Implementation and Inference}
Model Fitting: Discuss methods for fitting the log Gaussian Cox model with a negative binomial distribution, including Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) and variational inference.
Inference and Prediction: Explain how to perform inference and make predictions using the fitted model.
